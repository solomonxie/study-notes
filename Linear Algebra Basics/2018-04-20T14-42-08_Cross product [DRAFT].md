**So don't waste time on this unless having certain use of it.**

**REMEMBER: A CROSS PRODUCT ONLY HAS USE IN 3-DIMENSIONS, AND ORDER MATTERS, AND GIVES YOU A NEW VECTOR.**

List it again:
- Only work for 3-Dimensions
- Order matters
- Gives a new vector
